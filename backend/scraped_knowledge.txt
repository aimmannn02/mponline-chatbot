## What Does MPOnline Do?

MPOnline Limited is the Joint Venture company between Government of Madhya Pradesh and TATA Consultancy Services (TCS), established in 2006 to empower citizens and bring transformative improvements in public service delivery. With a strong focus on accessibility and citizen-centric services, MPOnline continues to drive digital innovation across the state.


Offering a comprehensive suite of services including G2C (Government to Citizen), G2B (Government to Business), B2C (Business to Citizen), and B2B (Business to Business) solutions, MPOnline also plays a vital role in sectors such as higher education, online assessment systems, and school education.
